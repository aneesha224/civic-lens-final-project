      Evidence and scope

      • The collection includes selected public speeches and Congressional Record remarks. It does not include every statement by a politician.
      • Coverage varies by person and year. A missing match means no match was found in the selected sources.
      • Analysis summarizes the linked text; it does not independently establish whether a factual claim is true.

      How to use Civic Lens

      Read the speech. Follow the topic.

      Choose a politician and statement at the top. Read the transcript on the left; click a highlighted topic cue or choose a topic on the right to compare related language across the selected sources. Analysis summarizes the selected speech and links its evidence. Civic Lens helps investigate claims in context; it does not decide whether a claim is true.
