      Evidence and scope

      • The collection includes selected public speeches and Congressional Record remarks. It does not include every statement by a politician.
      • Coverage varies by person and year. A missing match means no match was found in the selected sources.
      • Analysis summarizes the linked text; it does not independently establish whether a factual claim is true.
