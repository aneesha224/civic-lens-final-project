ANEESHA CHANDRA  ·  CIVIC LENS

01 · Motivation

Public language is easy to find one speech at a time.
Harder to follow across years.

Students, journalists, and civic researchers often have to collect statements, read them in isolation, and manually trace a policy idea back to its source.

The research friction

  • Campaign transcripts and legislative remarks live in different places.
  • A keyword hit needs surrounding text and a source link to mean anything.
  • Comparing years by hand makes context easy to lose.

The question Civic Lens helps investigate

Where does this politician discuss a topic, and what language appears in each source?

The app organizes a finite public record for exploration. It does not establish motive, truth, or a complete career history.
