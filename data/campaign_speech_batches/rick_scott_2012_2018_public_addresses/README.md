# Rick Scott public addresses and campaign speeches, 2012–2018

This batch has six complete speeches from non-Congressional public events. The source videos are from the Republican Governors Association, the Federalist Society, Florida National News, and FOX 4 Now. For each item, the original English YouTube auto-caption SRT and yt-dlp video metadata are preserved beside the extracted transcript. Cue wording is kept in source order without editorial correction; standalone non-speech cues are omitted.

- **Feb. 11, 2012 — CPAC address.** RGA source description dates the speech.
- **Nov. 16, 2012 — Federalist Society National Lawyers Convention address.** The Federalist Society description identifies the convention and Mayflower Hotel, and the speaker’s introduction is excluded.
- **July 20, 2016 — Republican National Convention speech.** RGA title names the speech as full; the stage introduction is excluded. The first caption overlaps the host’s final “Scott” and Scott’s opening “Good,” so the transcript retains only “good.”
- **Nov. 4, 2014 — gubernatorial reelection victory speech.** WFLA News Channel 8 footage runs from Scott’s post-introduction remarks through his final “let’s keep working”; emcee setup and reporter analysis are excluded. The final caption cue overlaps the two speakers, so only Scott’s last word is retained.
- **Apr. 9, 2018 — U.S. Senate campaign launch.** Florida National News identifies ODC Construction in Orlando and describes the event as Monday; its video was uploaded Tuesday, Apr. 10.
- **Nov. 6, 2018 — Senate campaign victory speech.** FOX 4 Now says it was delivered at Scott’s Naples watch party Tuesday night and was uploaded Nov. 7.

CSV transcript paths are relative to this batch folder.
