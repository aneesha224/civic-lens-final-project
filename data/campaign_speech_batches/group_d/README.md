# AWPC continuation speech batch

Two additional complete public commencement speeches from the Archives of Women’s Political Communication at Iowa State University. Each direct AWPC page is preserved unchanged beside its speech text extract.

The records are distinct from the events already present in the central manifest. The CSV uses the project schema; transcript paths are relative to this batch folder and should be remapped under `data/campaign_speeches/captures/` when merged. The central manifest was not edited.

## Records

- **Elizabeth Warren — Quinsigamond Community College Commencement Address – May 18, 2018** (2,210 words). Source: https://awpc.cattcenter.iastate.edu/communication/quinsigamond-community-college-commencement-address-may-18-2018
- **Nancy Pelosi — Commencement Address at UC Berkeley - May 17, 2014** (1,256 words). Source: https://awpc.cattcenter.iastate.edu/communication/commencement-address-uc-berkeley-may-17-2014
