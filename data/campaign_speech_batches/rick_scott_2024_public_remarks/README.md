# Rick Scott 2024 public remarks

This batch contains two ScottForFlorida video-caption transcripts. Each source video is linked from Scott’s official campaign page. The original YouTube English auto-caption SRT, parsed video metadata, raw source video page, and the official campaign page are preserved in `captures/`. Transcript text keeps caption cues in source order, excludes standalone music/applause cues, and does not correct automatic recognition or remove overlap.

## Turning Point Action People’s Convention

The captioned Scott-only speech is complete, from his opening at the mic through “God bless you” (about 21 minutes). The official campaign page says he delivered it at Turning Point Action’s “The People’s Convention” in Detroit. There is a delivery-date conflict: the official video was uploaded June 14, 2024; the campaign page is dated June 17 and its lead says he delivered the speech “today.” The event/convention program and the campaign’s date wording need reconciliation. The provisional CSV date follows YouTube’s video upload date; **do not count this record toward a target until its delivery date is verified**.

## DNC press conference in Chicago

The campaign page describes Scott’s remarks with Senator Ron Johnson at the Trump/Vance press conference during the DNC. It is dated August 20, 2024, and says the event was the previous day; the official channel video is also dated August 19. Its caption track contains Scott’s statement from the opening through the closing sentence. This record is a press-conference statement, not a convention speech.
