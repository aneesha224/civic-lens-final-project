# Additional low-coverage public-source records

Two Gary Peters records from complete CBS News *Face the Nation* broadcast transcripts. Both are televised interviews, not campaign speeches. The corpus already permits interviews as public-source records; formal speeches and public remarks remain the priority.

- **Nov. 30, 2014:** Joint interview with Thom Tillis; Peters joined from Detroit. All five CBS-labeled Peters turns (598 spoken words) are retained; host questions and Tillis turns are excluded.
- **May 19, 2024:** Interview with Margaret Brennan. All 11 CBS-labeled Peters turns are retained; interviewer prompts are excluded. The source misspells one turn label as `SEN. PETRES`; that source label is preserved.

Both source pages identify the broadcast date, and the full public-reader Markdown renderings are preserved beside the extracted transcripts. These are page renderings, not raw origin HTML. No transcript wording was generated. The two source URLs and IDs were absent from the canonical manifest at collection time. CSV transcript paths are relative to this batch folder; remap them to the main corpus `captures/` folder when merging.
