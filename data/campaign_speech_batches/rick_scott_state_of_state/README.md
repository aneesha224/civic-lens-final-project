# Rick Scott State of the State addresses

Five complete annual addresses are staged from non-Congressional public records:

- **Mar. 6, 2013:** Sessiontube video, English YouTube automatic captions. The captioned speech begins at 00:11 and continues through the closing thanks at 36:58.
- **Mar. 3, 2015:** Governing's complete text-and-video transcript. The address begins “Good morning President Gardiner…” and ends “God bless America and God bless the exceptional state of Florida. Thank You.” The source page is preserved as a reader-rendered Markdown capture; its embedded promotional link was removed from the transcript.
- **Jan. 12, 2016:** Sessiontube video, English YouTube automatic captions, from the opening greeting through the closing thanks.
- **Mar. 7, 2017:** News4JAX's uncut address video, English YouTube automatic captions. The transcript starts with Scott's response after the announcer introduces him; the original SRT retains the full recording. The date is corroborated by the Florida House video titled “FULL: 3.7.2017 - State of the State 2017” and the [Ballotpedia 2017 address listing](https://ballotpedia.org/State_of_the_state_addresses_(2017)).
- **Jan. 9, 2018:** Florida House of Representatives full-address video, English YouTube automatic captions; title and upload date identify the address date.

For each caption-based item, the extracted `.txt` retains caption wording in cue order, and the original English `.srt` is preserved beside it. Non-speech markers such as applause and music are omitted from `.txt`; no caption wording was corrected. `youtube_video_metadata.json` records each source video and the auto-caption method. The additional Florida House metadata file corroborates the 2017 event date.

CSV transcript paths are relative to this batch folder; remap them under `data/campaign_speeches/captures/` when merging. The record IDs and source URLs were absent from the canonical manifest at the time of staging.
