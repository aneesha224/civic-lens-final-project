LECOM 30TH ANNIVERSARY 
PROCLAMATION 
HON. MIKE KELLY 
OF PENNSYLVANIA 
IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES  
Tuesday, July 18, 2023 
Mr. KELLY of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I 
proclaim the Lake Erie College of Osteopathic 
Medicine (LECOM) as a pillar of the commu-
nity. 
Whereas, LECOM has been established 
since 1992 in Erie, Pennsylvania as a college 
of Osteopathic Medicine and now celebrates 
its 30th Anniversary; and 
Whereas, LECOM has grown to become the 
Nation’s largest medical college with cam-
puses in Erie, Pennsylvania, Greensburg, 
Pennsylvania, Bradenton, Florida, and Elmira, 
New York; and 
Whereas, LECOM has prepared and grad-
uated more than 16,000 students to become 
osteopathic physicians, pharmacy practi-
tioners, and dentists through programs of ex-
cellence in education, research, clinical care, 
and community service to enhance the quality 
of life through improved health for all human-
ity; and 
Whereas, LECOM has expanded its reach 
to include exceptional programs in graduate 
studies designed to provide scholars super-
lative education in the respective areas of 
study; and 
Whereas, LECOM is opening a new School 
of Podiatric Medicine and pursuing opportuni-
ties to open additional campuses, further dem-
onstrating their steadfast commitment to con-
tinue expanding healthcare education; and 
Whereas, LECOM offers a quality medical 
education at an affordable price with one of 
the lowest, private medical college tuitions in 
the Nation; and 
Whereas, LECOM provides students a 
choice of student-centered pathways that 
match the learning styles of individual students 
to promote success. 
Whereas, LECOM has been ranked by U.S. 
News & World Report as one of the top med-
ical colleges for graduating primary care physi-
cians; and 
Whereas, LECOM is the most applied to 
medical college in the United States; and 
Whereas, LECOM has the Nation’s only os-
teopathic academic health system with its own 
teaching hospitals, a physician network, and a 
full continuum of services for older adults; 
Now therefore be it resolved, that I proclaim 
LECOM as an acclaimed school of Osteo-
pathic Medicine in the Commonwealth of 
Pennsylvania. 
Be if further resolved, that during their 30th 
anniversary, LECOM is commended for the 
exceptional impact the organization has on the 
communities they serve and for their contribu-
tions to improving healthcare for all.

HONORING THE BRAVERY OF 
GICELA ALEMAN 
HON. COLIN Z. ALLRED 
OF TEXAS 
IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES  
Tuesday, July 18, 2023 
Mr. ALLRED. Mr. Speaker, on May 6 our 
North Texas community was left scarred by 
the shooting at Allen Premium Outlets. 
Nine people were killed, and 7 others were 
injured. 
But today, I recognize the brave story of the 
service of Gicela Aleman. 
An unsung hero who that day was shopping 
for new shoes at the outlet mall with her 
daughter, Adele. 
When the gunman opened fire, he severely 
wounded Preeti Patel, a mother and grand-
mother who was visiting family in North Texas 
and like so many others—was just trying to do 
some shopping. 
Amidst the chaos and violence, Gicela 
sprung into action. She grabbed Preeti and 
tried to slow her bleeding. 
After seeing that ambulances were strug-
gling to get through security, Gicela drove 
Preeti herself to get the care she needed. 
Preeti is alive today because of Gicela. 
An amazing story that deserves a hero’s 
praise. 
We thank her for her bravery, selflessness 
and representing the best of North Texas.

RECOGNIZING ROSA CART-
WRIGHT’S SERVICE TO THE CITY 
OF YPSILANTI 
HON. DEBBIE DINGELL 
OF MICHIGAN 
IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES  
Tuesday, July 18, 2023 
Mrs. DINGELL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to 
recognize Rosa Margaret Cartwright of Ypsi-
lanti, Michigan, on the occasion of her 100th 
Birthday. Her dedication to her family and 
local community over the last century is wor-
thy of commendation. 
Rosa was born on July 18, 1923, in Roa-
noke, Alabama to Eva and George Dozier. 
Rosa came to Michigan by way of Ohio in the 
1940s. During this time, she attended the Na-
tional Beauty Culturists’ League for Cosme-
tology and held the title of 3rd Vice President 
and Secretary to the League. Rosa also 
pledged Theta Nu Sigma sorority and is cur-
rently 1 of the oldest living sorority members. 
She has also held the title of Theta Nu Sig-
ma’s Ambassador for the State of Michigan for 
many years. 
After receiving her degree in Cosmetology, 
Rosa married her husband Benjamin in 1951. 
Together the couple had 4 children. A proud 
small business owner, Rosa was the owner 
and proprietor of Tony’s Beauty Salon for over 
70 years in addition to a small convenience 
store. Rosa is still an active community mem-
ber and has served in many city and local or-
ganizations, including the Eastern Star as well 
as serving as Choir Director and Vice Presi-
dent of Mount Olive Baptist Church. Rosa still 
meets up weekly with a group of avid game 
players, and until recently taught piano. 
Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me 
today in celebrating the 100th birthday of Mrs. 
Rosa Cartwright. We are so proud of her con-
tinued tenacity and spirit at such a well-sea-
soned age. She continues to be a pillar of the 
Ypsilanti community and is a true beacon of 
love and friendship to all family and friends 
that are lucky enough to be in her presence. 
Happy birthday to Rosa.

CONGRATULATING EMILIA 
LABRUYERE ON HER CLASS 5 
STATE CHAMPIONSHIP 
HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER 
OF MISSOURI 
IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES  
Tuesday, July 18, 2023 
Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise 
today to ask my colleagues to join me in con-
gratulating Timberland High School’s Emilia 
Labruyere on her victory in Missouri’s Class 5 
Discus State Championship. 
Emilia claimed the Class 5 title, becoming 
the Wolves’ first female track and field state 
champion. Her incredible, winning throw of 
42.14 meters matched both her personal best 
and school record-winning toss of the same 
distance a month prior. Emilia should be com-
mended for all her hard work throughout the 
past year and for bringing home the state 
championship to her school and community. 
Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing 
Emilia Labruyere for a job well done.
