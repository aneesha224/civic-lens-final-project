Sen. Bernie Sanders looked far ahead after his Super Tuesday losses, arguing that the Democratic race still had many states left.

In a speech in his home state of Vermont, the Democratic presidential candidate noted that his fight against front-runner Hillary Clinton is not yet over, with voters in 35 states left to cast ballots.
