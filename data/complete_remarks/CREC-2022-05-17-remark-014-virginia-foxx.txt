Ms. FOXX. Madam Speaker, I yield 
myself the balance of my time. 
Madam Speaker, the amendments in 
this en bloc will refocus the workforce 
development system on the individuals 
it was created to serve: jobseekers and 
employers. 
We must make sure that WIOA is 
helping all American jobseekers get 
the skills they need to compete in our 
modern economy. We must also make 
sure that WIOA programs are helping 
employers connect with skilled work-
ers, instead of saddling them with un-
necessary requirements that have no 
proven effectiveness. 
Madam Speaker, I urge my col-
leagues to support this amendment, 
and I yield back the balance of my 
time.
